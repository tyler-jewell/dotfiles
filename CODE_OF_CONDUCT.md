# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text at that link applies; this is the short form.

**Our standard.** Be respectful and assume good faith. Welcome newcomers, give
and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the
community. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, publishing
others' private information, and sexualized language or attention are not
acceptable.

**Enforcement.** Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits,
issues and contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or
permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge inappropriate.

**Reporting.** Report concerns privately through GitHub: use the repository's
**Security > Report a vulnerability** form and mark the report as a conduct
concern. Reports are handled confidentially, and reporters are protected from
retaliation.

**Scope.** This applies in all project spaces and when representing the project
in public.
